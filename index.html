Climate Org Map is a living, interactive map of the people and organizations working to protect life on Earth, and of the coalitions, projects, events, and ideas that tie them together. Instead of a static directory, it shows the movement as a network: see who belongs to which coalition, who already works together, and who could, then zoom in to find a group’s current projects, upcoming events, and the questions it is working through in the open. Switch to the geographic view to discover what is happening near you. What began as the Massachusetts Climate Organization Map now grows to host maps beyond MA, so that coalitions everywhere can see one another and act together. Every group keeps its own entry current, so the map stays accurate and belongs to the movement it serves.
